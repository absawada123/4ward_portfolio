About ClientLink

ClientLink is a lightweight CRM with PH‑friendly client records, notes, linked documents, and follow‑up reminders designed for service workflows and collections. See value, unpaid billings, and history at a glance to prioritize outreach and maintain relationships. It’s the straight‑to‑the‑point client hub for small teams.

Key Features

Client Profiles

Contacts, tags, and addresses with quick search.

Notes & History

Pin key notes and view past interactions.

Linked Documents

Keep contracts and files attached per client.

Follow-Ups

Schedule reminders to drive collections and renewals.

Insights

See value, unpaid, and overdue amounts at a glance.

Import/Export

Move client lists in and out via CSV.
