About LogSense

LogSense reveals where money goes by grouping income and expenses by category, with quick filters, date ranges, and side‑by‑side comparisons. Switch to graphs to spot trends and seasonality, then export summaries for reports or client updates. It’s a compact analytics layer tailored to cash‑based tracking.
