About Assethub Pro

AssetHub Pro is a clean asset register for acquisition date, cost, location, custodian, and manual depreciation tracking aligned with non‑tax bookkeeping needs. Keep receipts and warranties attached, label assets, and set status flags like In Use, Under Repair, or Disposed for better control and audit readiness. Simple exports make handoffs to accountants painless.

Key Features

Name, category, serial, location, and custodian in one view.
